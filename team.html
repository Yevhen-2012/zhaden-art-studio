Our Team

Zhaden Art Studio is a collective of architects, interior designers, and artists united by a vision of space as a form of art. We don’t just design interiors or buildings — we create atmospheres where every line, texture, and ray of light serves a deeper emotion and purpose.

Our strength lies in the intersection of disciplines. Architectural precision, artistic thinking, and design sensitivity allow us to move beyond convention and shape spaces that feel alive and meaningful. Each team member brings a unique perspective, but we work as one — with respect, inspiration, and a deep attention to detail.

We believe true design is not just about form, but about soul. And the heart of that soul is our team — the people behind every space we create.

HeElen Björklund - creative director

My name is Elen, and I see space as a living canvas. As a creative director, I don’t just shape interiors — I compose moods, dialogues, and quiet emotional rhythms through form, light, and texture.

My journey began with painting, and over time it evolved into architecture and interior design — but the core remained the same: storytelling through visual language. I believe in design that breathes — thoughtful, honest, and filled with meaning.

I’m inspired by silence, shadow, raw materials, and moments that can’t be repeated. In every project, I look for balance between structure and soul — between what is seen and what is felt.
Each member of Zhaden Art Studio is more than a specialist — they are a storyteller. Whether through structure, color, or composition, we craft narratives that resonate with the people who live, work, and gather in the spaces we design.

Our architects think like artists. Our designers shape with emotion. Our artists work with the spatial intuition of builders. This fluid exchange of roles keeps our process alive, dynamic, and deeply human.We thrive on dialogue — with each other and with our clients.

Collaboration is not just how we work; it's how we grow. From the first sketch to the final detail, we move together with curiosity, purpose, and a shared vision of excellence.Meet the minds and hands behind our studio — the team that transforms ideas into experiences, and spaces into stories.
